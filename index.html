How do I get a quote?
Call (256) 353-1460 or email kingagcy@att.net. It takes a few minutes, it's free, and there's never any pressure.
Can you review my current policy?
Yes — send your current policy to kingagcy@att.net and we'll do a free side-by-side review, flagging gaps and overpriced coverage.
Do you work with more than one insurance company?
We're an independent agency, so we shop multiple carriers for you and recommend the right coverage — not just the most expensive one.
What do I need to have ready for a quote?
Just the basics: for auto, your drivers and vehicles; for home, your address and the home's age. We'll walk you through the rest on the call.
Will you help me if I have a claim?
Absolutely. When something goes wrong you won't face the carrier alone — we stand with you through the claim, start to finish.
